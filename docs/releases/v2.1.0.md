## v2.1.0 — 與 iPad 版同一套設計 | The iPad design comes to the Mac

### 繁體中文

- **全新介面：跟 iPad 版一致。** 左側窄邊欄放目的地，上方一列放網址列、上一頁／下一頁與分頁；設定、選單、紀錄視窗都換成同一套配色。App 圖示也換成跟 iOS 版相同。
- **每個目的地各自有分頁。** 每個目的地最多 20 個分頁，⌘T 開新分頁、⌘W 關閉、⌘⇧T 重新開啟剛關掉的分頁、⌘[ ⌘] 上一頁／下一頁。最後一個分頁也能關掉，關掉後會顯示「沒有分頁」與「新分頁」按鈕。
- **自由調整面板大小。** 游標移到頁面右下角會出現圓弧把手，拖曳即可調整寬高；⋯ 選單裡的窄／中／寬仍可一鍵回到預設大小。
- **提示詞與職業組合。** 在傳送選單裡，對目的地長按、按右鍵或按 → 鍵，就能選一個提示詞再送出（例如「簡單解釋」「摘要」「翻成英文」）。設定裡可選 7 種職業組合（一般、學生、醫師、律師、研究人員、工程師、教師），並自由增刪修改，每個目的地也能有自己的提示詞。
- **三指輕點查詢（選用）。** 在設定裡開啟後，用觸控式軌跡板三指輕點任何文字，就能直接查詢游標下的字詞或選取的文字。
- **送出提示。** 文字直接送到目的地時，游標旁會跳出一個小提示，顯示送到哪裡、送了什麼。
- **設定重新整理：** 「從其他 App 傳來的文字」可選「上次使用」「固定目的地」或「每次選擇」，並可設定是否自動送出。
- 已安裝 v2.0.x 的使用者可以從「設定 → 更新」直接更新。

### English

- **New look, matching the iPad app.** A slim rail for destinations on the left and a top bar with the address field, back/forward and tabs. Settings, the picker and the records window share the same colors, and the app icon now matches iOS.
- **Tabs per destination.** Up to 20 tabs per destination. ⌘T opens a tab, ⌘W closes it, ⌘⇧T reopens the last closed tab, and ⌘[ / ⌘] go back and forward. The last tab can be closed too; the destination then shows "No tabs" with a New tab button.
- **Resize the panel freely.** Move the pointer to the page's bottom-right corner and drag the arc that appears. Narrow / Medium / Wide in the ⋯ menu still snap back to the preset sizes.
- **Prompts and profession templates.** In the send picker, press and hold a destination, right-click it, or press → to pick a prompt before sending (for example "Explain simply", "Summarize", "Translate to English"). Settings offer 7 profession templates (General, Student, Physician, Lawyer, Researcher, Engineer, Teacher) that you can edit, and each destination can have its own prompts.
- **Three-finger tap lookup (optional).** Turn it on in Settings, then tap any text with three fingers on the trackpad to look up the word under the pointer or the selected text.
- **Sent confirmation.** When text goes straight to a destination, a small chip next to the pointer shows where it went and what was sent.
- **Reorganized settings:** "Text from other apps" can use the last destination, a fixed destination, or ask each time, with an auto-submit option.
- v2.0.x users can update from Settings → Updates.
