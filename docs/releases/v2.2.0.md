## v2.2.0 — 網站清單、到別處問 | Site catalogue and Ask elsewhere

### 繁體中文

- **新增網站改成分類清單＋搜尋。** 約 70 個網站分成一般搜尋、AI 對話、學術與醫學、圖書館、工具書與翻譯、日常六類，包含 PubMed、UpToDate、UpToDate Expert AI、DynaMed、Cochrane、DeepSeek、Copilot 等；可用名稱或網址搜尋，按「加入」即可。
- **有搜尋網址的網站直接開結果頁**（例如 PubMed、Google Scholar、維基百科）；其他網站會把問題填進輸入框並送出。自訂網址裡放 `{q}` 也能當搜尋網址。
- **「到別處問」按鈕**：在瀏覽器列「在預設瀏覽器開啟」與「上一頁」中間。由查詢開啟的分頁可一鍵把同一個問題送到其他網站；已回答過的網站會直接切過去。
- **右側圖示列新增「＋」**，直接開啟「新增網站」。
- 修正：設定裡「＋ 新增網站」按鈕太靠左。

### English

- **Add a site is now a searchable catalogue** of about 70 sites in six groups (general search, AI chat, academic & medical, library, reference & translation, everyday), including PubMed, UpToDate, UpToDate Expert AI, DynaMed, Cochrane, DeepSeek and Copilot.
- **Sites with a search address open their results page directly** (PubMed, Google Scholar, Wikipedia …); other sites get the query typed into their box and sent. A custom address with `{q}` works as a search address too.
- **Ask elsewhere**: a toolbar button between Open in browser and Back sends the same query to another site; sites that already answered it switch to that tab.
- **A "+" on the rail** opens Add a site.
- Fixed: the "+ Add a site" button in Settings hugged the left edge.

### 安裝 | Installation

1. 下載 `Peekabrowser_2.2.0_aarch64.dmg`（Apple Silicon）或 `Peekabrowser_2.2.0_x64.dmg`（Intel）| Download the DMG for your Mac
2. 打開 DMG，把 Peekabrowser 拖曳到「應用程式」| Open the DMG and drag Peekabrowser to Applications
3. 已安裝舊版的使用者可以從「設定 → 更新」直接更新 | Existing users can update from Settings → Updates
