## v2.0.6 — 更新後自動重新授權 | Re-grant permissions automatically after updating

### 繁體中文

- **改善：從舊版更新後不必再手動移除、加回「輔助使用」。** v2.0.5 起改用 Apple 簽署，舊版留下的授權紀錄會失效。這版第一次啟動時會自動清掉失效的紀錄並跳出系統詢問，只要在系統設定裡打開 Peekabrowser 的開關即可。
- **改善：「螢幕錄製」同步清理。** 失效的紀錄會一併清掉，下次截圖時 macOS 會重新詢問。
- **改善：設定 →「⌘C ⌘C 快速傳送」的按鈕改為「重新授權」**，之後若權限失效，按一下就會重新詢問。
- **簡化：DMG 不再附 `Install.command`**，直接拖曳到「應用程式」即可。
- 已安裝 v2.0.x 的使用者可以從「設定 → 更新」直接更新。

### English

- **Improved: no more removing and re-adding Peekabrowser under Accessibility after updating.** Since v2.0.5 the app is Apple-signed, which invalidates grants left by older versions. On first launch this version clears the stale entry and shows the system prompt; just switch Peekabrowser on in System Settings.
- **Improved: Screen Recording is cleaned up too.** The stale entry is removed, and macOS asks again on the next screenshot.
- **Improved: Settings → "⌘C ⌘C Quick send" now has a "Re-grant" button** that asks again whenever the grant stops working.
- **Simplified: the DMG no longer includes `Install.command`**; just drag the app to Applications.
- v2.0.x users can update from Settings → Updates.

---

### 安裝 | Installation

1. 下載 `Peekabrowser_2.0.6_aarch64.dmg`（Apple Silicon）或 `Peekabrowser_2.0.6_x64.dmg`（Intel）| Download the DMG for your Mac
2. 打開 DMG，把 Peekabrowser 拖曳到「應用程式」| Open the DMG and drag Peekabrowser to Applications
