## v2.1.2 — 打字時不再誤觸三指查詢 | Three-finger lookup no longer fires while typing

### 繁體中文

- **修正：打字（特別是注音）時常誤觸三指查詢。** 打字時手掌或拇指碰到觸控板，會被當成三指點按，查詢游標下的字。現在按鍵或修飾鍵後 1 秒內的三指點按會被忽略。
- 已安裝 v2.0.x 或 v2.1.x 的使用者可以從「設定 → 更新」直接更新。

### English

- **Fixed: three-finger lookup fired by accident while typing (notably with Zhuyin input).** Palms or thumbs brushing the trackpad while typing were read as a three-finger tap and looked up the text under the pointer. Taps that start within 1 second of a key press or modifier change are now ignored.
- v2.0.x and v2.1.x users can update from Settings → Updates.

---

### 安裝 | Installation

1. 下載 `Peekabrowser_2.1.2_aarch64.dmg`（Apple Silicon）或 `Peekabrowser_2.1.2_x64.dmg`（Intel）| Download the DMG for your Mac
2. 打開 DMG，把 Peekabrowser 拖曳到「應用程式」| Open the DMG and drag Peekabrowser to Applications
