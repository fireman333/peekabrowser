## v2.0.1 — 修正自動傳送時側邊欄立刻收起 | Fix sidebar closing right after auto-send

### 繁體中文

- **修正：** 啟用「預設用第一個 Destination 開啟」後，若游標在螢幕右側時按 `⌘C ⌘C`，側邊欄會一打開就立刻收起。
  - 原因：側邊欄打開前就已排定的「自動隱藏」檢查，在確認時沒有重新判斷「游標是否進過面板」，也沒檢查面板是否剛被重新打開，所以把剛打開的面板關掉了。
  - 現在自動隱藏的排定與確認用同一套規則：手動打開（`⌘C ⌘C`、自動傳送、截圖、快捷鍵、選單列）時，要游標進入面板、離開後才會收起。舊的隱藏檢查遇到面板重新打開會直接作廢，打開後 0.8 秒內也不會自動隱藏。
- **修正：** 面板是否顯示改為明確追蹤，不再依視窗座標推測。舊做法在剛啟動時會誤判為「已顯示」，可能導致第一次左緣顯示沒有反應。
- 已安裝 v2.0.0 的使用者可以從「設定 → 更新」直接更新。

### English

- **Fixed:** with "send to the first destination by default" on, pressing `⌘C ⌘C` while the cursor was on the right side of the screen closed the sidebar right after it opened. A hide check queued before the panel opened was confirmed without re-checking whether the cursor had visited the panel or whether the panel had just been reopened. Scheduling and confirming now use the same rule, stale checks are discarded when the panel is reopened, and there is a short grace period after opening.
- **Fixed:** panel visibility is tracked explicitly instead of being inferred from window position, which looked "visible" right after launch and could make the first edge reveal unresponsive.
- v2.0.0 users can update from Settings → Updates.

### 安裝 | Installation

1. 下載 `Peekabrowser_2.0.1_aarch64.dmg`（Apple Silicon）或 `Peekabrowser_2.0.1_x64.dmg`（Intel）| Download the DMG for your Mac
2. 打開 DMG → 雙擊 `Install.command`，或拖曳到「應用程式」| Open the DMG → run `Install.command`, or drag to Applications
3. 手動安裝時執行 `xattr -cr /Applications/Peekabrowser.app` | If installing manually, run `xattr -cr /Applications/Peekabrowser.app`
