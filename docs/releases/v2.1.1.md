## v2.1.1 — 設定開關看得出開或關 | Settings switches show on and off clearly

### 繁體中文

- **修正：設定裡的開關，開和關的底色看起來幾乎一樣。** 關閉時改成淺色底，開啟時維持藍綠色，深色與淺色模式都看得出差別；開啟「增加對比」時關閉狀態會用更深的灰色。
- 已安裝 v2.0.x 或 v2.1.0 的使用者可以從「設定 → 更新」直接更新。

### English

- **Fixed: settings switches looked almost the same on and off.** The off track is now a light neutral fill while on stays teal, so the two read differently in both light and dark mode; with Increase Contrast on, the off track uses a darker gray.
- v2.0.x and v2.1.0 users can update from Settings → Updates.

---

### 安裝 | Installation

1. 下載 `Peekabrowser_2.1.1_aarch64.dmg`（Apple Silicon）或 `Peekabrowser_2.1.1_x64.dmg`（Intel）| Download the DMG for your Mac
2. 打開 DMG，把 Peekabrowser 拖曳到「應用程式」| Open the DMG and drag Peekabrowser to Applications
